Termilly on your phone

Three steps, once per phone. Everything stays on your own network.

1. Install Termilly's certificate

Download the certificate

  1. Tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings: at the top is Profile Downloaded. Tap it, then Install (twice). Not there? General › VPN & Device Management.
  3. Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings: turn Termilly on. This one is easy to miss.

Android: open Settings, search for CA certificate (Encryption & credentials › Install a certificate › CA certificate, not "VPN & app" or "Wi-Fi") and pick Termilly.cer from Downloads.

2. Open the app

Checking whether this phone trusts Termilly

Open Termilly

If the phone ever warns the connection isn't private, go back to step 1. Don't continue past the warning.

3. Add it to the Home Screen

iPhone: in Safari, Share › Add to Home Screen. Open Termilly from there and pair it.

Android: in Chrome, ⋮ › Add to Home screen. Open Termilly from there and pair it.
