Termilly on your phone

Three steps, once per phone. Everything stays on your own network.

1. Install Termilly's certificate

Download the certificate

iPhone: tap Allow, then open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap the Termilly profile and Install.

Then Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn Termilly on.

Android: open Settings, search for CA certificate (under Encryption & credentials › Install a certificate) and pick Termilly.cer from Downloads.

2. Open the app

Open Termilly

If the phone says the connection isn't private, step 1 isn't finished.

3. Add it to the Home Screen

iPhone: in Safari, Share › Add to Home Screen. Open Termilly from there and pair it.

Android: in Chrome, ⋮ › Add to Home screen. Open Termilly from there and pair it.
